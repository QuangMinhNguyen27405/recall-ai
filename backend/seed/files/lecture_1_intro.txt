Biology 101 - Lecture 1: Introduction

Cells are the basic unit of life. All living organisms are made of one or more cells.
Key themes:
- Structure and function are closely related.
- Energy transformation is essential for life.
- Homeostasis keeps internal conditions stable.

Important terms:
- Prokaryote: cell without nucleus.
- Eukaryote: cell with nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.
- Metabolism: all chemical reactions in an organism.
