Biology 101 - Lecture 2: Membrane Transport

Cell membranes are selectively permeable and regulate movement of substances.
Transport types:
- Passive transport: diffusion, osmosis, facilitated diffusion.
- Active transport: requires ATP to move substances against gradients.

Examples:
- Sodium-potassium pump is active transport.
- Oxygen moves into cells by diffusion.
- Water movement depends on solute concentration differences.
